# Prior work and selected question

The completed shared-complex-quotient caller experiment regressed its two-size
logical32 aggregate 2.23% versus fresh OFF; it remains rejected, commit afe9e3f647.
Its source, captures and decision are unchanged.

The direct-finish primitive report is explicitly unqualified because the
intended fresh OFF and candidate executables both linked ON. Its post-capture
audit recovered distinct immutable OFF/ON artifacts but did not execute a
replacement timing run. It recommends a separately declared actual-caller
study. There is no qualified primitive speedup and no full SDK result for this
exact arithmetic. The current original inverse source matches its input
byte-for-byte. This study reuses the exact helper, including all original
34-test qualification inputs, without changing arithmetic or repeating the
old primitive measurement schedule.

Other mechanisms inspected this turn are not new candidates:

- Hrabar's Waterloo thesis, https://uwspace.uwaterloo.ca/items/43cc12b9-cb03-42bd-a01c-4b0ed5e12403,
  describes digit clustering and factor recoding. The local 21 October global
  pair census/search-floor and 28 October factor-recoding studies already
  evaluate related mechanisms. Their negative/inconclusive outcomes remain.
- https://arxiv.org/html/2610.09767v1 describes truncated PMNS internal
  reduction. The local 23 October six-coefficient PMNS native study already
  tried schoolbook and two paired-product variants, with losing multistream
  arithmetic. Its larger-modulus results do not establish a BN254 improvement.
- Dual-CIOS scaling, component-lane packing, streamed complex B, lazy linear
  arithmetic, FIPS and B1 elimination already have completed local work.
  They are not reimplemented here.

No outside implementation is adopted. The retained direct reducer's bounds
are a local derivation; its GCD background is Pornin's binary-GCD work.
