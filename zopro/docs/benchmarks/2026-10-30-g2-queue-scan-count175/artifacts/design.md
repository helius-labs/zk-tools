Accepted borrowed G2 take_from_queue pops from the tail until the first destination already in the pending affine batch. Entries below that blocked item are not inspected. The alternative scans the bounded queue once from tail to head, swap-removes every currently free entry, and leaves blocked entries. Moving an already inspected blocked tail entry to an earlier slot is safe: busy bits only become set during this drain, so that entry cannot become free until execute.
