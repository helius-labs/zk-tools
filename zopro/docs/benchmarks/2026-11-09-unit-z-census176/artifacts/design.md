# Actual-proof unit-Z mixed-add census

The previous unit-z-mixed176 prototype passes native/generic curve oracles and emitted-code checks. This stage measures no latency. It counts how often actual complete SDK proof requests enter the standard mixed-add specialization, using current adopted bitmap-reset source, native settings and fixed worker placement.

Diagnostic-only field hooks count add_affine entries, right identity, left identity, and ordinary/equal/opposite branches split by unit/nonunit left Z. Two identities have their original precedence. Classification reuses the original u2/s2 field results; the original arithmetic and all returns are unchanged. Counters are two nine-slot AtomicU64 arrays plus an enable flag, gated by a private cfg. They are disabled while loading and for three warm proofs, reset/enabled around one complete fresh sdk.prove call, then disabled before export. This includes solving/proving/internal verification/serialization as exposed by sdk.prove; counts do not isolate MSM or a critical path. Diagnostic atomic overhead makes timing uninterpretable.

Build a coherent new diagnostic field/core/prover/zolana/SDK using the current 176-source manifest and accepted flags, including dense bitmap reset, original field formulas, prefix and carry settings. Only field lib.rs/curve.rs and the census child change. No prototype optimization is enabled. A new focused counter test checks exact branch conservation, disabled/reset boundaries, both curves, all 53 workers across 1/4/16/32 pools and four callers sharing one eight-worker pool. Existing field/core/Window/prototype tests and decodes are inherited without rerun.

Fixed cases: all eight original shapes and merge54 at 32 workers; primary also at 16 workers and solo. Eleven fresh processes, three warm proofs plus one counted proof each: 44 SDK requests, 11 new independently Gnark-verified exports. No per-case exclusion, repeat, timing inference or new CPU/RAM/throughput claim. All eleven SDK processes precede eleven offline Gnark commands.
