# Signed coefficient bound

Let B=2^52. Every IFMA operand digit is in [0,B), including the 27-bit top digit of the unsigned input sum. Low and high halves of each product are in [0,B). At one slot one complex row contributes at most two terms P-Q to the real coefficient, hence absolute value less than 2B, and at most two terms S-P-Q plus one shifted sum-carry digit to the imaginary coefficient, hence absolute value less than 5B. A raw row and cancellation row together add less than 10B to either coefficient. There are three rounds total. The only other operation before final repacking adds a canceled coefficient divided by B to the following slot. Two such operations add less than 100 total under an inductive 40B bound. Therefore all accumulation intermediates are below 40B+100, and the declared looser 128B=2^59 bound holds, below signed64 range.

Cancellation computes three low halves, bounded by B, and their sums/differences have absolute value below 3B. Each cancellation digit is masked to 52 or26 bits before reuse. A digit sum can have 53 bits; its top carry is at most one and is added as one shifted normalized input-sum digit. This is required for BOTH the input row and the modulus row. The first implementation of the interpreter missed the input-row carry and failed stage comparisons; original source and failure logs are retained. No timings were taken before this correction.

First two canceled low coefficients are exactly divisible by B. The arithmetic shift is thus exact, including negative coefficients. In the last round the divisor is 2^26. Coefficient3 contributes a multiple of 2^130 to the quotient and is deliberately omitted. Repacking uses arithmetic shifts and normalized low26-bit fragments of coefficient1/2; its three digits reconstruct the quotient modulo2^130. Each repacked low digit is bounded by B+2^34 and the top by2^34. Input-sign and cancellation-centering corrections operate on a fixed number of digits below B and remain within signed64 range before normalization. This proves representation safety, not the parent coordinate closure: the latter follows from equality with its unchanged Comba REDC and inherited range model.

The direct integer recurrence, separate IFMA-half interpreter, final repacking and signed-input/cancellation-centering corrections all agree with the parent exact Comba oracle on canonical/lazy corners, 4096 fullwidth changing pairs and digit boundaries. Tests are finite validation in addition to this bound; they do not constitute an exhaustive domain proof.
