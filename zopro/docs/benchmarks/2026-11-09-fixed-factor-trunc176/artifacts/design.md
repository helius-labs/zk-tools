New question: multiplying a normalized raw field value x<2p by a fixed **ordinary canonical** integer w<p can use a precomputed quotient and truncated products instead of a full Montgomery product. The output represents x*w modp, preserving x's R256 or R260 radix without a codec because w is ordinary, not Montgomery encoded. The initial target is bounded FFT root factors, not arbitrary varying MSM operands or a per-key table. No paper/library speedup transfers.

Primary background: Shoup fixed-operand multiplication (https://www.shoup.net/ntb/ntb-v2_5.pdf; FLINT documented source algorithm https://flintlib.org/doc/ulong_extras/shoup.html) and truncated vector products in Didier et al., https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.18129. Their word-size/RSA implementations are not this five-limb algorithm. Recent FLINT documentation explicitly notes generated content, so rely on exact locally derived identities/bounds and independent integer checks rather than its prose as a proof. No external implementation body is copied. New code is Rust.

For B=2^52, R=B^5=2^260, precompute Q=floor(wR/p). Split x,Q,w,p into five normalized digits. Approximate H=floor(xQ/R) by retaining full terms with i+j>=5 and only the high half of terms with i+j=4. Normalize this high convolution into five digits. Its missing contribution E is nonnegative and below9: independent digit bounds give Emax=9-10/B+1/B^5. Since x<R, xw/p=H+E+x*epsilon/R with0<=epsilon<1, hence H<=floor(xw/p) and 0<=xw-Hp<10p<R. This exact remainder is recovered by the LOW FIVE digits of xw-Hp, discarding the final wrap borrow. Discarding that borrow is intentional modular-R arithmetic, not a signed representative fed into an unsigned field API.

Use a second small quotient k=floor(r4*mu/B), mu=floor(B/(p4+1)), where r4 is the top digit of r=xw-Hp. This underestimates r/p. Bound r-kp<2p by checking the exact modulus-specific integer inequality

    R + t4max*(R-mu*p) < B*p,   t4max=floor((10p-1)/B^4).

Indeed r-kp < p + B^4 + r4*(B^4-mu*p/B). The inequality bounds the last two terms below p. Compute k with one IFMA high half, subtract its five digit products using five 64-bit low multiplies and signed carries. Return ordinary normalized raw range[0,2p). All coefficients and carry extrema must have exact signed64 safety bounds. No invalid host Fp object or approximate residue is allowed.
