# Fixed sparse interpolation and wide-word recipe

Let B=2^52 and A(X), B(X) have five input digits in[0,B). Their ordinary product has coefficients w0..w8 bounded by[1,2,3,4,5,4,3,2,1]*(B-1)^2. A fused sum doubles these bounds. These coefficient inequalities also cover valid field inputs; no field constraint is needed for the interpolation argument.

Evaluate at0,+1,-1,+2,-2,+3,-3,+4,infinity. Pinf=w8. Forx=1,2,3:

    e(x²)=((P(x)+P(-x))/2 - w0 - x^8*w8)/x²
          =w2+x²*w4+x^4*w6
    o(x²)=(P(x)-P(-x))/(2*x)
          =w1+x²*w3+x^4*w5+x^6*w7

The even schedule is h14=(e4-e1)/3, h49=(e9-e4)/5, w6=(h49-h14)/8, w4=h14-5w6, w2=e1-w4-w6. Form E16=w0+16w2+256w4+4096w6+65536w8 and o16=(P4-E16)/4. For the odd cubic, d14=(o4-o1)/3, d49=(o9-o4)/5, d916=(o16-o9)/7; dd149=(d49-d14)/8, dd4916=(d916-d49)/12; w7=(dd4916-dd149)/15, w5=dd149-14w7, w3=d14-5w5-21w7, w1=o1-w3-w5-w7. All divisions are exact integer divisions, not modular approximations. The symbolic interpreter checks each coefficient's divisibility and the nine final identity vectors. It propagates signed linear bounds using the doubled triangular coefficient bounds above.

Each wide value is represented by three words q0+q1*B+q2*B²: the lower two digits are normalized[0,B), and the upper digit is signed. The conservative symbolic guard is |value|<2^126. Inputs/evaluations/interpolation and positive constant scales must all satisfy this guard; finite observed maxima do not replace the symbolic proof. Upper digits are then below2^22 in magnitude, leaving signed-i64 headroom for their additions, shifts up to16bits and multiplication by a constant<=65536. Lower-word additions/subtractions and normalization carries fit signed-i64. Left shifts model modulo64 low-word shifts followed by masks; explicit neighbor-word transfers preserve the complete integer. Negative top words shift arithmetically.

The evaluator uses signed Horner additions and shifts forx in{+-1,+-2,+-3,+4}. Absolute evaluation bounds use sum|x|^j*(B-1): at4 this is341*(B-1)<2^61. All Horner partials and their shifts are bounded by the corresponding partial positive sum. Point0/infinity have52-bit operands and need two IFMAs. At the other seven points, decompose a=al+B*ah and b=bl+B*bh with al,bl in[0,B), signed ah,bh. Then ab=al*bl+B*(al*bh+ah*bl)+B²*ah*bh. The low product needs two IFMAs; three signed64-bit products provide the corrections. Each high part has magnitude at most341, so cross products and their sum, including the low-product high half, are below683B<2^62 in magnitude. This is not a free52-bit multiply. Normalize the middle coefficient into a signed top word.

Wide addition/subtraction uses three lane operations followed by two arithmetic carry/mask/add steps (nine operations). A fixed power-of-two shift or exact power-of-two division uses three word shifts, two neighboring-word masks, two transfers and two OR/adds (nine operations). Positive non-power constant scaling uses four IFMAs, one signed64-bit top product, and three normalization operations. These are source-recipe counts and may overcount operations an optimizer removes; they do not describe optimized instructions or a lower bound for other schedules.

Odd exact division is low-to-high. For odd d<=15, let v=d^-1 modB and V=d^-1 mod2^64. Compute q0=lo52(a0*v); c0=hi52(q0*d); n1=a1-c0; q1=lo52(n1*v); c1=hi52(q1*d)-(n1>>52); n2=a2-c1; q2=low64(n2*V). Here n1>>52 is arithmetic. The first two exact quotient digits use four IFMAs. Since the entire wide value is divisible byd, n2 is divisible byd. Its true signed quotient fitsi64 by the wide bound, so the modulo64 product gives that unique two's-complement quotient. This costs four IFMAs, one modular64-bit product and four other operations. Powers of two in a composite divisor are removed first. The standalone signed-division population supplements this argument; it is not a native instruction qualification.

Final w_i are nonnegative. Scattering their three normalized words into radix52 coefficients creates at most two digits belowB and one small top contribution per output coefficient, hence coefficients<3B. An eleven-coefficient array handles full-width fused sums; valid[0,2p] field inputs have zero coefficient10 because2p<2^255 and their full product sum is below2^511. The common Montgomery cancellation skeleton therefore receives a narrower coefficient envelope than the previously checked32B input bound. This stage checks the assembled product against independent BigInt coefficients/full multiplication, and mathematical Montgomery quotients against an independent radix inverse. It does not run or requalify the inherited reduction recurrence or a native field implementation.

Adding the inherited five-row50-IFMA cancellation skeleton to the new convolution/interpolation is an optimistic work ledger. Common reducer ALU work, moves, constants, code size, live register allocation and spill costs are excluded and must not be called zero. A dense rational inverse is not used as a native plan. Finite model populations, native SIMD execution, and proof verification are distinct evidence.
