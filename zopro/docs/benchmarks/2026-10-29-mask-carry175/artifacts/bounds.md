# Branchless final carry normalization

The original five CIOS product/cancellation rows, mixed48bit repacking and fused correction remain byte-identical. Only the final four-step ripple changes, in a separately named private method; original methods remain in the same module. No four-lane/global field dispatch, production consumer, key/image, heap or lookup table changes.

Let B=2^52 and t[j]=r[j]+B*q[j], j<4. First form v0=r0, vj=rj+q(j-1) for1<=j<4 and v4=t4+q3, independently. This preserves the represented integer. Conservative original CIOS term bounds place all coefficients below2^61: at most six half terms per row, five rows plus bounded carry; final mixed repacking shifts at most four bits. The pre-repacking bound31B yields below512B=2^61. qj<=511<B, so v1..v3<=B+510<2B; the only remaining carries are bits.

Set gj=(vj>=B), pj=(vj==B-1). The exact carry recurrence is c1=g1, c2=g2 OR(p2 AND c1), c3=g3 OR(p3 AND c2). A generate remains a carry even after incoming1, a propagate produces a carry iff incoming1, and all other coefficients produce none. Outputs are v0, v1 modB, (v2+c1)modB, (v3+c2)modB, v4+c3. They equal ordinary ripple exactly, including arbitrary cascades. No probability assumption, cold-path or zero-carry fast branch. Intrinsics use five vector comparisons, mask Boolean operations and three masked vector additions. More work and register pressure can erase any dependency benefit.

The old24Oct algorithm uses the same independent first transfer but conditionally ripples on any cascade. It lost the blanket component and prefix-only integration. This candidate replaces that correction algorithm with exact always-evaluated mask propagation; it does not rerun the old kernel. The paired-cancel28Oct quotient lookahead, FIPS29Oct row ordering and accepted CIOS cancelled-low carry identity remain separate and unchanged.
