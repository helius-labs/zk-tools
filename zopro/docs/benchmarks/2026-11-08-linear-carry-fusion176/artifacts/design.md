# Canonical-anchor sum with one carry pass

Question: can the X finishing expression square(slope) - (Ax + Bx) avoid normalizing the intermediate sum? This is a new local unnormalized-limb type and consumer, not the earlier canonical_add substitution, whole-integer late-centered arithmetic, or normalized lazy-linear quotient algorithm. For B=2^52, each normalized input digit is in [0,B). Let numeric 0<=A<2p and 0<=X,Y<p. The five raw sum digits S_i=X_i+Y_i are in [0,2B-2]; they are NOT normalized IFMA operands, even though sum S_i B^i=X+Y<2p. A private owning type must prevent their use by multiplication, export, or general arithmetic. Subtraction alone computes v_i=A_i-S_i+carry_i, then d_i=v_i mod B and carry_(i+1)=floor(v_i/B). Carries may be -2 at internal positions, not just borrow bits. Whole A-X-Y is in (-2p,2p), so after five digits final carry is -1 or0 since2p<2^260. The low five normalized digits encode that integer modulo2^260. If negative, add2p and normalize, discarding the exact wraparound above five digits. Result is the same representative in [0,2p) as original A.sub(X.add(Y)). Neither a modular multiplication nor an IFMA operand contract changes.
