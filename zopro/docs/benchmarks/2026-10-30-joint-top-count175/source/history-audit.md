# History and scope

The previous partial-window turn was progress: correct restricted local-pair counts, with only179/181 genuine-partial generic additions saved. Its necessary gate passed but that layout was not selected. No unchanged timing, fixture oracle or production suite repeats here.

Before this design, a content search checked joint/top/component sharing, top rings/orbits, coalesced tops and old SRE searches across benchmark READMEs and design/history files. The following overlap is preserved:

- `2026-10-21-ryzen-research/sre-bounded-window-opportunity`: bounded adjacent full-width scalar-window searches, original ordinary scalar routes and different large shapes. Its gate failed. It did not share the two narrow Phi2 components' joint pair classes.
- `2026-10-21-ryzen-research/global-window-search-floor`: serialized all-window-pair search is too costly. No such search or pair-index dictionary is repeated.
- `2026-10-24-ring-indexed-simd-component`: whole-scalar radix64 ring recoding, unit/sign transforms and indexed trees failed. This design does not recode scalars, add windows or transform incoming key points.
- `2026-10-27-fused-top`: combining separate K/H input slices in one top reduction failed its full gate. Those points are separate inputs; the new joint classes share identical original G2 points across the two existing component marginals.
- `2026-10-29-g2-gls4-coalesced-top177`: coalesced groups in the highest window of the rejected four-component GLS4 decomposition failed its primary SDK gate. The new design keeps accepted Phi2 decomposition, ordinary ten windows and two parts. It reduces input arrivals by joint digit equality, in addition to changing first-tree batching. The coalesced-independent model is retained only as attribution, without claiming a runtime win.
- `2026-10-30-g2-balanced-sdk175`: balanced eleven-window geometry removes the old indexed top but fails its whole-proof gate. It is not mixed in.
- `2026-10-29-stream-ibs175` and `2026-10-26-front-end-counters`: arithmetic/backend observations already exist; another generic frontend recording is unnecessary. Outlining trials are closed. The retained service trace attributes11.712342ms median covered CPU to primary indexed G2 leaves, not removable wall time or proof of a faster replacement.
