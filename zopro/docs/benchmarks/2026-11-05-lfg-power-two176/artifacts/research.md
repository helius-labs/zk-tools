Architecture decision: those retained shifted-point arrays conflict with the user's no-key-copy/no-large-LUT universal memory requirement. Do not implement them. A bounded tile/Horner variant would need transient3P, repeated per-window weighted reductions and complete accounting. It cannot use the paper's single shared reduction or online bound, and earlier fixed2P/3P/hexagonal/binaryring candidates remain closed. The paper alone does not justify rerunning those. This screen should close the literal precomputation route, while preserving any independently new low-memory mathematical lead as unimplemented.
