# Complemented fused product difference

Let p be either BN254 prime, R=2^256 or2^260, and a,b,c,d be normalized stored integers in[0,2p], with4p<R. Set n=R-1-c and X=ab+nd+d=ab-cd+Rd. Complement each radix52 digit independently, using48bits for the final R256 digit. Initialize the ordinary unsigned fused CIOS accumulator with d rather than zero. Both multiplicands n and d fit IFMA digits; n is deliberately not a public below2p field value.

With q=(-X p^-1) modR, T=(X+qp)/R. The quotient equals that for signed Z=ab-cd because Rd is divisible byR. Thus U=T+p-d=(Z+qp)/R+p. Since -4p²<=Z<=4p² and0<=q<R, 0<p-4p²/R<=U<2p+4p²/R<3p. X>=0 and X<=4p²+2pR<3pR, so T<4p. Add p-d into the repacked coefficients before final signed radix52 normalization; one original conditional subtraction of2p returns normalized output below2p, congruent to (ab-cd)/R modp. The lazy representative may differ from the old implementation.

Unsigned CIOS and its accepted cancelled-low ceil carry identity remain exact. Each IFMA digit product has52-bit low/high parts. At each row, each accumulator coefficient receives at most two input low halves, two previous-column high halves, a modulus low and a previous modulus high, plus bounded preceding coefficient/carry. Across five rows a conservative bound below2^61 leaves signed64 headroom. Mixed48bit repacking shifts only masked48bit coefficients by4; nonmasked high parts remain below the same conservative signed64 bound. Signed correction adds/subtracts one digit and carry; its exact nonnegative total below3p<R ensures terminal carry zero. The exact model checks every quotient, packed integer and returned representative against independent BigInt arithmetic and tracks all coefficients.

This removes the serial borrow chain for2p-c, but adds a seed and a post-reduction correction. It keeps150fused IFMA sites. No lower latency or register-pressure improvement is assumed. It is distinct from Gauss (no product sharing), raw signed product accumulation, quotient lookahead, canonical-anchor product-subtraction and persistent lazy representations. Only an additive private field helper is proposed; existing APIs and core callers stay unchanged in the component screen.

Background review: [Efficient Modular Multiplication](https://eprint.iacr.org/2021/1151.pdf) discusses redundant Montgomery ranges when4p<R. The complement/seed/correction identity above is derived and tested here; no paper speedup is transferred. Prior shared/phased/nonnegative Gauss, product-sub, mask carry and paired/triple quotient studies remain closed.
