Offline analysis requires one sampled SDK PID and zero lost records. It conserves cycles periods, records actual per-event counts/periods and derives off-CPU intervals from own-TID SWITCH OUT/IN pairs. The analysis clips intervals to the observed enabled sample span, reports boundary/unmatched records, and separates preempted, runnable and sleeping switch-outs where trace state is available. A same-TID sched_yield preceding a nonpreempted switch-out within 100 us is an association, not proof of exclusive wall-time cost. Own-task attachment cannot observe every other task or distinguish all sources of competition. Futex entry counts do not imply actual blocking or successful waits.
