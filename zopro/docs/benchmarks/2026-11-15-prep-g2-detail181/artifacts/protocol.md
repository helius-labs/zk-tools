# Preparation and G2 dependency diagnostic

Question: which actual preparation passes grow with worker count, and does G2
finish late because its window work is unfinished or because a completed parallel
collection returns late? Parent C7a capture180 establishes the discrepancy but its
8 slots cannot resolve these alternatives. This is instrumentation, not a speedup.

Use accepted source plus the retained coarse observer, unchanged arithmetic,
work geometry and joins. Add six preparation spans (reclaim, C drop, public
correction, W canonicalization, A gather, B gather); G2 grouping, group-point
reduction, scalar splitting, window collection and final combination; per-window
and per-part spans, merging and weighted summation. Fixed 74 cache-line slots,
no per-leaf trace, TLS attribution, new scheduler or retained key tables. Borrow
one request-owned observer across the original joins. Total logging after solve
remains inside SDK latency. Neither callback spans nor collection gaps are
exclusive CPU or automatically removable time. No model change or adoption.

Cheapest test: local functional preflight OFF/ON at 4 workers then a fixed screen
at 16/32 with REF/OFF/ON, three independently written byte-exact backings per
binary, nine rotated/reversed blocks, 31 timed requests plus three primes.
Require every export independently Gnark verified and canonical ledger union
from 55664. Require complete nested unique intervals, exactly 11 windows and two
parts when the phi path is used, and no arithmetic fallback hidden by the reader.
REF/OFF and ON/OFF paired nine-block rank1..7 intervals and each backing median
must lie in [0.95,1.05] to attribute a trace to accepted execution. All controls
are reported even on failure; do not extend capture or relax gates.

Local outcomes only qualify the observer and compare local dependencies. They
cannot explain C7a causally. If calibrated, deploy the same instrument to one
owned C7a32/96 run with identical matrix and guards, then terminate/clean it.
Preparation allowance 45 minutes, local capture 5 minutes; simplify or explain
any overrun. No new generic campaign framework, no successful-run replay.

screen; backing-sensitive regression separately diagnosed. Old per-task interval
observer perturbed96 and is not reused. Weighted groups, frontend overlap,
fused-product overlap and solver-only search restriction remain closed recipes.
This experiment adds stage completion evidence without changing those policies.
