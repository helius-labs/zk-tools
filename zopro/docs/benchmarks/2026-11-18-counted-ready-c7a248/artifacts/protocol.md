# Count-first solver construction on C7a

The question is whether counting unique edges before allocation reduces the construction reservation while preserving warm proof latency. OLD is the exact previous solver enabled, REF is accepted d523, and OFF and ON share corrected build 2d47. Preflight must confirm the expected activation in all shapes and worker counts and verify every export with Gnark.

Use one c7a.24xlarge, 96 physical cores, SMT disabled. Pin the first 32 or 96 cores. Use the same three input images and requests as experiment 246. After 24 preflight processes run three rotated/reversed blocks of plain latency across the four arms for 1×2, 2×2 and merge54. Each process primes three times and measures 151, 51 or 17 proofs respectively. Then collect the separate 96-core 1×2 OFF and ON observer, grouped cycles/instructions stat, and dual sampling panels, three blocks and 251 timed proofs per process. Record all blocks. Total 114 processes, 9,798 timed proofs, 10,140 calls, one independently verified export per process. Seed with 64,307 distinct digests. No extension or exclusion.

In each of six contexts compare OLD and ON preflight graph dimensions, require equal roots/leaves/edges and strictly lower accounted construction bytes. Report retained bytes separately. For the construction change to pass the descriptive screen, require median paired ON/OLD warm latency ≤1.03 in each context and max plain RSS ≤OLD×1.10+8 MiB. Report whether merge54 maximum RSS actually fell. Preserve separate comparisons of ON to OFF and REF, including CPU time and RSS. Flag OFF/REF calibration outside [1/1.03,1.03]. A primary 96-core improvement attributed to readiness requires ON faster than OFF and REF in all three blocks and median paired CPU ratios ≤1 against both with passing calibration. Reserve adoption for the broader shape qualification.

Require 100% scheduled PMU counters, inferred PID/TID, event identity, correct mmap, period and timestamp, at least 1,000 samples/event and ≤5% unknown, zero lost. Assess each profile panel against its own plain observer using a factor of 1.05. Plain proof timing remains interpretable independently. Report perturbation. Reported RSS is whole-process high water, construction charges are reserved capacity. No minimum RAM inference.

Run exclusively on one owned TTL-terminated VM, no ingress, 30 GiB/no swap benchmark unit. Use the prior authenticated transport and host-side re-verification, then verify instance, volume, bucket and multipart cleanup.
