# Decision

Do not integrate this G1 ternary reduction into the SDK. All four declared main
cases fail the component condition. Tile384 is unresolved near parity; tile511,
tile2048 and group35973 regress. The smaller mixed-tail case has a positive
10.3509% paired estimate, wins31/31 and passes its per-case condition. Preserve
that result without treating it as qualification for the observed primary
indexed geometry or for all grouped jobs.

The earlier small caller added Q and R through two complete streaming calls.
This component instead compares all layers of an unweighted reduction, where
the existing binary implementation already batches independent pairs across
groups. Its overhead and inversion structure differ. Small two-update gains do
not transfer automatically. The new formula removes some inversion stages, but
adds field products, guards and eight-word records. Whole-process samples are
consistent with more work in the candidate layer; they do not isolate arithmetic,
gathering, cache traffic or allocator costs causally.

Four profiles have3157 samples, one PID each and no lost records. At2048 points,
binary reduce_layer receives72.1% of sampled period; the candidate ternary layer
receives77.6% of its separate recording. Scalar inverse plus inverse_nonzero
receive11.1%/8.9% respectively. These percentages use whole-process samples,
including setup/checks, and are not removable wall-time bounds or a PMU cycles
comparison. The larger candidate kernel outweighs its reduced inversion share
in the measured caller. Tile384 is approximately parity despite fewer allocation
calls. Allocation call count alone is not an optimization objective.

Record scratch is modest per job but still larger, and retained binary scratch
must be counted too. Tile2048 has346128/360416 bytes of declared common+binary+
ternary+fixed endpoint capacity. Group35973 has6162264/6239984 bytes. Neither is
peak process RSS, transient allocator peak, key storage or minimum RAM. There is
no implemented admission-policy change, and no pressure/phone claim. The private
caller was intentionally tested before production resource integration.

Close this eight-word-record, ternary-every-eligible-layer mechanism. Do not
repeat it with the same source, tune thresholds after the result, promote the
synthetic tail case, or run full SDK/AWS merely to search for a different verdict.
Future investigation needs a distinct mechanism and a caller tied to its actual
usage. The census and this result together make the next selection more specific:
eliminating inversion stages by increasing per-point field work is insufficient
at the real large G1 caller sizes. Revisit whole-proof CPU work and arithmetic
dataflow, with a history audit before selecting a new experiment. No new candidate
is qualified by this experiment.

unchanged. Independent proof count15553 is unchanged: this is a curve-caller
component experiment with native/projective and independent Arkworks checks,
not a proof capture. The original all-eight CPU10x goal remains active,0/8.
