# Paired cancellation algebra and bounds

Let B=2^52, p odd, and I=-p^-1 modB². The coefficients t0/t1 are not normalized after adding two product rows. Their low two normalized digits are U0=t0 modB and U1=(t1+floor(t0/B))modB. For I=I0+BI1, the exact quotient digits are q0=(U0I0)modB and q1=(floor(U0I0/B)+(U0I1 modB)+(U1I0 modB))modB. The latter has no dependency on the result of cancelling q0. IFMA ignores high operand bits, so the native input to the second low product can remain unnormalized after adding floor(t0/B). The final quotient is masked to52bits where required by the scalar model.

Applying q0 and q1 sequentially to the existing coefficients gives (T+(q0+Bq1)p)/B². Each cancellation uses the retained exact identity (t0+q*p0)/B=ceil(t0/B)+floor(q*p0/B), including t0=0. Thus its cancelled low IFMA is still omitted. Two such blocks process the first four52bit rows, followed by the original final52bit (R260) or48bit (R256) row. The mixed last carry uses high52((q<<4)*p0), because q<2^48.

Both primes are below2^254, so inclusive operands<=2p have five radix-52 digits with top digit<2^48. Every raw or modulus IFMA contribution is below B. Across a complete fused operation, at most100 raw low/high contributions and45 modulus low/high contributions enter state coefficients. Discarding coefficients cannot increase their sum. A deliberately conservative induction bounds the pre-packing sum by145B plus five carry terms each<=256; this is below146B and closes a256B bound. The mixed final top temporary starts at zero after the second block and receives at most three products of operands with top digits<2^48, so t5<3*2^44; shifting it left4 is safe. Other mixed packing terms mask48bits before shifting. Final carry propagation and all packed intermediates fit below2^61. The exact interpreter independently checks that limit at every stage; observed maxima are recorded, not substituted for this bound.

Let R=2^256 or2^260. For ordinary input product T<=4p², exact REDC=(T+Qp)/R<p+4p²/R<2p. For fused T<=8p² and R260, the same result is below2p. For fused R256 it is below3p; the retained conditional subtraction of2p gives a representative below2p, including the inclusive2p input corner. All five output digits are normalized, with the same Montgomery radix and existing fused correction. The independent integer model checks every paired quotient/block, final repacking, exact representative and field interpretation. Native tests compare field values with Arkworks and check digit/range constraints.

The template adds four IFMAs per multiply:104 instead of100; sum2 has154 instead of150. It uses a seven-coefficient temporary instead of six, plus an independently computed second quotient. One extra inverse digit costs eight logical constant bytes per field, excluding emitted vector constant materialization. No heap allocation, key transformation, point table or format change is introduced by the kernel. Compiler frames and lifetime overlap can change; this is not a proof of unchanged whole-prover RSS.

Native component results justify caller-level investigation only. Mixed ordinary multiplication regresses; mixed fused and uniform ordinary contexts improve. Static instruction/frame counts and sampled cycles do not isolate the causal contribution of shorter quotient dependencies or predict proof latency. The all-case aggregate is an equal-weight component metric, not a prover work estimate.
