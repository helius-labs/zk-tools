# Bounds for the shared symmetric square

Let B=2^52. Every input digit is in [0,B), and the complete inputs a,b are in [0,2p]. Splitting each raw digit product into lo and hi gives each part below B. Cross-square terms and all ab terms are doubled by 64-bit shifts of lo/hi, giving values below2B; no widened input is sent to IFMA.

For either output, a coefficient receives at most two square halves from each neighboring product position, plus the corresponding ab halves. A conservative row bound charges8B for raw additions per coefficient. Montgomery cancellation charges at most2B per coefficient, plus a low carry. Across at most five rows, charge10B+64 per row. Induction gives every live coefficient below50B+320<64B=2^58. The low carry for52-bit cancellation is at most64. The final48-bit cancellation carry is at most1024. Repacking its lower48-bit pieces left by4 produces each coefficient below2B+1024; final radix52 propagation adds at most64 to its neighbor. All values stay below2^58 and every 64-bit vector addition/shift is exact. The integer model records intermediates and separately checks each cancellation against the exact integer quotient; these are sampled checks, not the source of the universal bound.

At row i, square products with the other index j>=i contribute once for a diagonal or twice otherwise. Earlier rows already contain the symmetric lower-triangle terms. The ab contribution includes all five indices in every row. Thus the complete raw integers are X=a²+b² and Y=2ab+b². Each completed low coefficient is canceled before advancing; later raw terms occupy higher weights and do not alter canceled digits. The independent row-identity check compares the vector coefficient integer with (before+m*p)/2^s for s=52, or s=48 in the last mixed row. The cancellation inverse ensures divisibility.

X<=8p² and Y<=12p². Standard Montgomery cancellation adds an integer m*p with 0<=m<R, so the unreduced result is nonnegative and strictly below p+12p²/R. For Fq and either R256 or R260, p<R/4, hence the result is strictly below4p. One subtraction of2p when needed returns a value in [0,2p). The unchanged reduce_fused routine performs this correction after radix normalization. R256's last48-bit row and R260's last52-bit row are independently covered. Inputs at exactly2p are included.

The helper is private to this arithmetic prototype. These bounds do not qualify a new serialized Fq2 representation, new curve formulas, process RSS, compiler stack depth or a portable backend.
