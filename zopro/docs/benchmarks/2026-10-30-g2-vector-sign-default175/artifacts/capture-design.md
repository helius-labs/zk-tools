# Final-artifact performance protocol

Declared before capture. This assesses the changed handwritten-module/default-dispatch artifact. It does not repeat the unchanged private candidate to repair its failed 16-core control calibration. That historical full gate remains false.

Three fixed binaries: `accepted` is the frozen accepted production SDK (20fde19f…); `OFF` is the previously measured vector-sign private SDK (fbd37c67…); `ON` is the new default candidate. OFF is an implementation reference, not an identical arithmetic baseline. Its ratios against accepted are descriptive, not calibration of identical code. No new fresh control is claimed.

Use all eight supplied reference shapes plus 54→1 at 32 workers, and primary at 16 physical cores. Freeze 31 rotated/reversed blocks of all three variants and all ten cells. Each process primes three proofs, then measures 151 primary requests or 31 requests for other shapes; all requests verify internally. Export one new proof per process and independently verify all exports with exact image/public/key bindings and uniqueness against the actual 26,292-proof ledger. Preserve every observation; no exclusions, extension, parameter sweep, changed threshold or unchanged reroll.

Compute paired process-median ratios; report median, ranks 10–22 of 31 (nominal marginal coverage 97.055%, without family-wise guarantee) and wins. Final-artifact gate: ON/accepted primary32 upper bound <1 and at least 21/31 wins; all other ON/accepted upper bounds <=1.02; all ten ON/private-reference upper bounds <=1.02; ON max RSS <=1.05 times each reference in each cell. For the original eight shapes, require equal-log aggregate ON/accepted upper <1 and >=21 wins; ON/private aggregate upper <=1.01. There is no minimum positive effect threshold. Report the historical and final gates separately.

After latency, collect six gated perf-stat panels and six fixed-period 16M user-cycle profiles: primary16/32 × all three variants. Require grouped event qualification, at least 1,000 samples per profile, <=5% unknown weighted samples, exact binary MMAP/PID/TID ownership, valid CPU affinity, every decoded sample period 16M, no frequency mode and zero lost records. Sampling supports relative CPU-work attribution; it does not establish absolute cycle coverage or causes of wall-time changes.

All helper/analyzer/publisher code and inputs must be built and pinned before timing. During capture, only poll the confirmed live workflow and communicate status. On terminal success, analyze once and independently review raw receipts and archives without repeating successful proofs, verifiers, tests or profiles. Publish positive and negative results, preserving the original objective and CPU/Metal comparisons on all eight shapes. Production remains unchanged until a distinct final engineering/adoption review, including new-artifact functional/resource checks if the performance gate passes.
