# Decision

Close this exact hybrid without adoption. All24 candidate/fresh-OFF paired
median ratios exceed1. The G1 and G2 logical32 aggregates regress against both
controls, as does the all24 aggregate. All48 nonregression and6 gain checks fail;
four control-calibration failures remain visible. All18 profiles qualify.

This is an explicit new integration decision following the old global
signed-pair52 failure, not a retroactive pass of that trial. Its body is the
qualified single-output kernel, not the separately rejected joint-complex
algorithm. Restricting it to R256 avoids the known uniformR260 loss, but does
not make complete point callers faster. Invariant-operand primitive timings
were insufficient evidence for changing-operand caller performance.

Fewer IFMA sites are accompanied by more total instructions and stack-addressed
vector rows. Dynamic instructions rise17-18% for G1 and31-32% for G2 in measured
panels. Cycles also rise, even while instructions/cycle can improve. This is
additional useful-path execution cost, not evidence of a DRAM-capacity problem.
Counters/profiles do not isolate a causal cost for each instruction or spill.

No compiler/layout/inlining/width/worker/curve subset sweep or unchanged reroll.
No full SDK candidate is selected. Keep the original all-eight CPU10x goal,
universal low-memory scope, accepted production176/SDK6d83 and canonical34929.

The next hypothesis must reduce the complete arithmetic/dependency/lifetime
path and qualify variable complete callers before any proof-time claim.
