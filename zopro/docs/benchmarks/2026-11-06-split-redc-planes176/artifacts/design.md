# Split product and reduction coefficient planes

Prospective mathematical/work screen, before implementation or native timing. Accepted source176, original field1200 and SDK6d83 remain unchanged. All-eight CPU10× goal is active/unmet. No candidate adoption or speed claim.

Evidence: accepted-dependencies176 reuses all 18 saved caller profiles/9 decodes (119,638 samples), with exact ELF/MMAP instruction mapping. In the OFF G2 block at +0x8aa2 (about 32–33% of all samples in each G2 panel), a selected connected calibrated register-only chain has 28 IFMAs and one add: weight114 with measured 4/2 weights versus113 at inherited4/1. This is partial dataflow, not measured block latency or proof that all stalls lie on that path. It makes fewer-IFMAs/parallel-final-carries an insufficient next hypothesis.

For the first four radix52 CIOS rows keep two six-coefficient nonnegative planes P and Q instead of one T. P receives only original a*b_i (+c*d_i) low/high product halves. Q receives only Montgomery q_i*p low/high contributions and cancelled-low carry. They share the quotient frontier z=P[0]+Q[0]. Set m=(-z/p0) modB as before. Add ceil(z/B)+floor(m*p0/B) to Q[1], and the original m*p_j contributions for j=1..4 to Q. Shift BOTH planes by one coefficient, discarding their cancelled low coefficients. Immediately before row4, merge P[j]+Q[j], j=0..4, and execute the original final radix52 or mixed48 row, final normalization and fused correction unchanged.

Invariant: the unnormalized polynomial value of P+Q is exactly the original CIOS state after each completed row. Since every coefficient contribution is nonnegative and P+Q equals the ordinary raw coefficient state, each plane is individually bounded by that sum. The integer frontier cancellation uses the same exact ceil-carry identity already accepted. No new mathematical reduction quotient is introduced. This is not independent complex output planes, two shared-operand products, raw Gauss/pair52, paired104-bit cancellation, row-quotient precomputation or FIPS anti-diagonal product scanning: products and reductions keep distinct accumulator dependencies until the frontier instead of immediately sharing t[j]. No novelty theorem is claimed.

Nominal product/reduction IFMA counts are unchanged (100/150). Additional vector work is at most four frontier adds plus five coefficient merges; the first frontier has Q=0 and may fold away. The plane separation adds six live vector coefficients and may increase stack traffic. These costs must be retained. No heap buffer, copied key/point, table, extra plan, pool, representation change or C/assembly rewrite.
