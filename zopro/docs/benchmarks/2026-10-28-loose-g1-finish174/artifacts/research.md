The implemented identity is derived from the current affine formula:
U=s², X=U-S, Y=s(ax+S-U)-ay. Instead of a forward weighted record or a new
quotient estimator, local 2p/p offsets keep intermediate integers nonnegative
and bounded below R. Unchanged nonfused multiplication consumes one operand
below5p. Two conditional output subtractions canonicalize each coordinate.
The precise ranges and Montgomery scaling are in bounds.md. No external
implementation, new persistent table or circuit execution plan is copied.
