A primary-source search for variable/nonuniform Pippenger windows returned
the standard Lambdaworks fixed-width source and hardware cost-model papers.
No new paper algorithm or performance result was imported. The local signed
digit identity is proved by telescoping: each span adds the preceding unsigned
bit as incoming carry and subtracts its own sign bit times the next radix.
The interior corrections cancel for variable widths too. Fr<2^254 and coverage
through bit254 make the final sign bit zero. Unit tests independently reconstruct
all five320-bit integer words, in addition to generator/point tests.
