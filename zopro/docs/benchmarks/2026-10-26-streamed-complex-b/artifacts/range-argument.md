The streamed sum uses B=2^52. For normalized input digits and carry c in {0,1}, s=b0[i]+b1[i]+c lies in [0,2B-1]. Its new digit s mod B and carry floor(s/B) exactly match the old five-limb precomputation, by induction from c=0. The sums are below 4p<2^256<B^5, so the final carry is zero. Summed A is unchanged. Every product operand, signed accumulator update and cancellation digit is thus identical to old shared CIOS. Neither reducing the sums modulo p nor replacing the signed accumulators is allowed.

Each row adds at most two signed low/high contributions to x coefficients and at most four to y coefficients, with each product half in [0,B). Reduction adds at most two positive product halves per coefficient. Five rows, signed low-carry propagation and zero-initialized accumulators fit the conservative 32B bound before the last mixed-radix packing.
