# Signed square difference in the ordinary Fq2 basis

Question: can the real component of the existing native complex square avoid the general field preparation `(a+b)*(a-b)` by evaluating the symmetric signed raw product `a²-b²`, followed by one signed Montgomery reduction? The imaginary component `2ab` remains exactly the ordinary path. This changes neither the Fq2 basis nor key/point representation.

For normalized numerical operands 0<=a,b<=2p, T=a²-b² lies in [-4p²,4p²]. Montgomery cancellation chooses 0<=k<R, so U=(T+kp)/R lies strictly between -p and2p when4p<R. Both BN254 primes and R256/R260 meet that condition. Add p only to negative U; the result is normalized, nonnegative and strictly below2p. No unsigned-product claim applies to T itself.

Emit each unique limb pair once, double off-diagonal signed differences, and cancel integrated rows in six coefficients. The low cancellation carry is SIGNED ceil(t0/2^w), with w52/48. A signed right shift in the carry and mixed-radix repacking is essential. IFMA sees the low52 bits of t0 when forming the correction; the correction itself is nonnegative and fits w bits. Product limbs are nonnegative. Model the exact signed coefficients and prove fixed-width safety with conservative interval propagation before relying on intrinsics.

Necessary correctness stage: exact integer row model for both primes/radices; independent closed REDC oracle; Cartesian boundary, radix/power-of-two, opposite/zero, full-width deterministic random and changing-output-chain inputs. Native implementation, if justified, needs checked and optimized tests against original field arithmetic and independent Arkworks results, including inclusive2p endpoints and raw normalized bounds.
